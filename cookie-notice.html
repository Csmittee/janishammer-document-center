Who we are

Janis Hammer Co., Ltd (Janishammer), operating SayCheer, Chonburi, Thailand. Contact: info@janishammer.com

What this covers

The cookies that Google Analytics sets when you visit saycheer.janishammer.com. We set no other cookies. We do not use cookies for advertising.

What the cookies do

They let us count visits and see which pages are useful, so we can improve the site. Cookie names: _ga and _ga_HB2ZXSZY0Z. How long they last: up to 2 years (Google's default); the clock restarts each time you visit.

What Google receives

Information about your visit, such as the pages you open, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location. Google processes it for us and may store it outside Thailand. We do not receive your name or your email from Google Analytics.

Your choice

The cookies are set only if you tap Accept in the bar at the bottom of the page. If you tap Decline, or do nothing, they are not set. You can change your mind at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page, or by deleting cookies in your browser. We remember your choice in your browser's storage (a small stored value, not a cookie).

How long we keep the data

How long Google Analytics keeps the data: event data is kept for 2 months and user-level data (linked to the cookies) for 14 months. The 14 months start again each time the same visitor returns.

Your rights

Ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@janishammer.com
